# codex-micro-easter-egg
The hint was in the video when he talked about it being a fidgit toy.

Open the Codex Micro settings page.
Hold the analog stick off-center and trace a smooth circle.
Keep rotating in one direction for just under two complete turns (1.9 turns).
Complete it within 6 seconds, with no pause longer than 1 second.
Reversing direction resets progress.
